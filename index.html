Recipe of the day

One-Pot Tomato Pasta

Comfort food without a mountain of dishes.

Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 20 minutes

Servings: About 4, depending on portions

What You'll Need

  • 12 ounces pasta
  • 1 can diced tomatoes (14–15 ounces)
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups broth or water, plus more as needed
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper
  • Grated cheese, optional

Let's Make It

  1. Add pasta, tomatoes, garlic, oil, seasoning, and broth to a large pot.
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer.
  3. Stir often and cook until pasta is tender, about 10–15 minutes depending on shape.
  4. Add a splash of water if the pot gets too dry.
  5. Taste, adjust seasoning, and top with cheese if desired.

Cooking times vary by pasta shape. Make sure the pasta is fully cooked.
